### Promt tạo vật phẩm
```
Create a single kawaii hand-drawn game item: **[ITEM NAME]**.

Visual style:

- Cute kawaii hand-drawn illustration
    
- Thick, smooth, dark-brown outline
    
- Soft rounded shapes with no sharp corners
    
- Warm, harmonious pastel color palette
    
- Slightly imperfect organic lines to preserve a handmade feeling
    
- Simple, clean details suitable for a cozy educational mobile game
    
- Gentle soft shadow directly underneath the item
    
- Front-facing or subtle three-quarter perspective
    
- Centered composition with generous padding around the item
    

Consistency requirements:

- Canvas size: 512 × 512 pixels
    
- The item should occupy approximately 70% of the canvas
    
- Match the same outline thickness, lighting direction, perspective, proportions and color treatment across the entire item collection
    
- Produce exactly one isolated item
    

Background:

- Transparent background
    
- No environment, floor, frame or decorative background
    
- No text, letters, numbers, labels or logos
    
- No watermark
    

Avoid:

- Photorealism
    
- 3D rendering
    
- Pixel art
    
- Anime character style
    
- Black outlines
    
- Sharp edges
    
- Strong highlights
    
- Heavy or realistic shadows
    
- Complex textures
    
- Extra objects
    
- Cropped or partially hidden item
```
